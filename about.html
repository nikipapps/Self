A little about me

My name is Gregory Papp, and I'm developing my skills in web design and information technology. I enjoy learning how websites are put together and discovering how a few lines of code can turn an idea into something people can use.

As I learn, I'm trying to build a strong foundation instead of rushing through the details. Practicing, reviewing mistakes, and improving my work are all part of the process.

My interests

One of my interests is raising geckos. I like learning about animals and providing consistent care. It takes observation and responsibility to notice what an animal needs.

I also enjoy video games when I have time. Games can offer imaginative worlds, challenges, and opportunities to think strategically. Both hobbies give me a break from schoolwork while keeping me curious.

Why I'm learning web development

HTML and CSS are useful starting points because they teach me how content and visual design work together. I want to continue learning, become more confident with technology, and prepare for a stable career in IT.

How I like to learn

  • Break a large task into smaller steps.
  • Practice a new skill by building something real.
  • Test my work and correct mistakes.
  • Keep learning instead of giving up when something is difficult.
